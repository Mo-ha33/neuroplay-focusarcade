Solar System Science Module - Grade 3 STEM Curriculum
=====================================================

Unit 1: Our Solar System
-------------------------

Learning Objectives:
- Students will identify the 8 planets in order from the Sun
- Students will describe key characteristics of each planet
- Students will understand the difference between inner and outer planets

Key Facts:
1. Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet
2. Venus is the hottest planet due to its thick atmosphere
3. Earth is the only planet known to support life
4. Mars is called the "Red Planet" because of iron oxide on its surface
5. Jupiter is the largest planet with a Great Red Spot storm
6. Saturn is famous for its beautiful ring system
7. Uranus rotates on its side, making it unique
8. Neptune is the windiest planet with speeds over 1,200 mph

Assessment Questions:
Q1: Which planet is closest to the Sun? A: Mercury
Q2: What is the largest planet in our solar system? A: Jupiter
Q3: Which planet is known as the Red Planet? A: Mars
Q4: What planet has the most visible rings? A: Saturn
Q5: Which planet is known for supporting life? A: Earth
Q6: What is the hottest planet? A: Venus
Q7: Which planet rotates on its side? A: Uranus
Q8: What is the windiest planet? A: Neptune

Hands-On Activity:
Create a scale model of the solar system using different sized balls.
Mercury = marble, Venus = tennis ball, Earth = tennis ball, Mars = golf ball,
Jupiter = basketball, Saturn = soccer ball, Uranus = softball, Neptune = softball
